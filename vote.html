❓ How voting works
🔐 Is my vote anonymous?

Absolutely. Your vote is encrypted and stored without any link to your identity. Only the fact that you voted (not your choice) is recorded in your history.

🛡️ What data is stored?

We store your admission number only to prevent double voting. Your candidate selection is anonymized in a separate database.

👁️ Can election officials see my vote?

No. The system uses split‑view encryption — no single person can trace a vote back to you.

🚫 Can I vote twice?

No. Once you cast your vote, your admission number is marked for this election. Any further attempt is blocked.

📅 What if I miss the deadline?

Voting closes at the date shown in election details. After that, the “vote” buttons are disabled and no further submissions are accepted.

🗳️ Can I change my vote?

To ensure integrity, votes are final. Please review your choice before confirming.

📈 When are results published?

Results are released after the election end date and time. They will appear on the dashboard and may be announced officially.

🏆 Are results live?

No. To avoid influencing voters, results are frozen until the election closes. After that, a tally is shown.

📋 Where can I see detailed counts?

A final results report will be available in the “past elections” section once the election is over.

⚙️ How is my vote secured?

We use Firestore security rules and server timestamps. Each vote triggers a write that is logged, and the total count increments atomically.

📱 What if the app crashes?

Your vote is confirmed only after Firestore acknowledges the write. If it fails, you'll see an error and can try again safely.

🔁 Can I see my vote receipt?

A timestamp appears in “your history” as proof of your participation, but not your candidate — that keeps it anonymous.
